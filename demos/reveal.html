Reveal

At rest, every bubble sits at the viewer's eye (translateZ equal to the perspective), so it can't be seen. Bubbles near the pointer drop back into place, revealing them in a small pool. If you've asked your system for reduced motion, they stay hidden.

On a touch screen, tap and the pool stays open where your finger was (pointerLeave: "hold"). Dragging over the stage moves it, since touchDrag is set to the stage; scroll the page from outside it.
